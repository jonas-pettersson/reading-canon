# Vision: Reading Canon

## Purpose

Reading Canon helps readers focus on books that are worth reading rather than simply finding more books to read.

The application transforms a carefully curated canon of literary and intellectual works into a personal and shared reading companion that helps readers discover, prioritize, read, and reflect on significant books.

## Vision

To create the best personal reading companion for readers who value quality, cultural significance, and thoughtful reading.

## Core Idea

Reading Canon is built around a curated canon rather than a comprehensive catalogue.

Its purpose is not to help users discover every book ever published. Its purpose is to help readers identify and engage with books that are worth reading.

The curated canon is one of the product's primary assets and differentiators.

## Target Users

### Primary User

The curator of the collection.

### Secondary Users

Friends and invited readers who want to explore, read, and discuss books from the canon.

## Product Principles

### Curated, Not Comprehensive

The collection remains editorially curated.

Users may recommend additions, but the canonical collection remains under the control of the curator.

### Reading Quality Over Reading Quantity

The application should encourage thoughtful reading rather than optimize for consumption metrics.

### Readers Own Their Journey

Each reader maintains personal reading status, priorities, ratings, ownership information, and reflections.

### Excellent Usability

The application should be enjoyable and intuitive enough that users prefer it over maintaining a spreadsheet.

### Progressive Enrichment

The collection should remain useful even when metadata is incomplete.

Additional information can be added over time without disrupting the reading experience.

## Long-Term Direction

Reading Canon should evolve into a platform where readers can:

- discover worthwhile books
- manage their reading journey
- record reflections
- share recommendations
- discuss books

while preserving the curated and editorially controlled nature of the canonical collection.
